## Remarks

Use the names as keys of [`FormatTemplates`](../headersformattingoptions/formattemplates/) to change how a header is written, or add them to [`RenderingHeaders`](../headersformattingoptions/renderingheaders/) to choose which headers are written. The names of contact and task fields are in [`Contact`](../mhttemplatename.contact/) and [`Task`](../mhttemplatename.task/).

## Aspose::Email::Bounce namespace

The Aspose::Email::Bounce namespace groups the BounceResult class and DSNAction enumeration, which together enable developers to interpret bounce messages and delivery status notifications programmatically. Use these types to automate handling of undeliverable email in your applications.
